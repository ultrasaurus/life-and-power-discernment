## Minutes from Meetings on Sexual Abuse

> Friends Meeting at Cambridge considers any sexual involvement of an adult with a child to be abuse of the child by the adult. Such behavior must be condemned.
>
> — Minutes of Cambridge Monthly Meeting (MA), April 5, 1987

> Our Meeting is a community of people seeking to perfect their love of God and of all living beings. We support each other in this quest through our silent worship together and in our relationships with each other.
>
> While we accept our human imperfections in interacting with each other, we are bound together by the trust that comes from our commitment to treat each other with love, compassion, and respect. Verbal, physical, and sexual harassment are actions which destroy this trust and require vigorous counteraction.
>
> If anyone in our Meeting feels harassed by another member/attender, the Meeting needs to be informed, usually through Overseers, in order to act to support all concerned, prevent further occurrences, and avoid isolation of any person.
>
> — Minute on Nurturing Trust in Our Community, Mt. Toby Meeting (MA), Sixth Month, 1989

> Women and men in our Meetings are struggling to come to terms with sexual abuse. This year Friends from Salem Quarter formed a worship group, Quaker Survivors and their Allies (QSA). We are concerned with inner healing and outward change in our communities. Even though Quakers are firmly rooted in the Peace Testimony that "utterly denies outward war," Friends have not corporately addressed violence or abuse in the home.
>
> Friends must begin to honestly examine the underlying reason for sexual (and emotional) abuse in our midst. Perhaps then all of us can learn to love with mutuality and respect. We ask Monthly Meetings to consider the following queries:
>
> 1. How would our lives be different if survivors of sexual abuse could count on every person in their monthly meeting as an ally?
> 2. How does the meeting handle current perpetrators of sexual assault (with children) in our meeting?
> 3. How do we bring God's healing into the darkness of secrecy, elusive memories, shame and denial?
>
> — Minute from Salem Quarter, New England YM, 23, Seventh Month, 1989

> Rochester Friends are united in wishing to welcome each person who comes to worship with us.
>
> We affirm loving relationships among mutually responsible and consenting people. We oppose sexual relations between adults and children, which by their nature cannot be mutually responsible and consenting, and which are necessarily harmful.
>
> Rochester Friends Meeting stands opposed to sexual violence and all forms of human violence, wishes to be attentive to the concerns of adult survivors of child sexual abuse, wishes to provide for the protection of children, and wishes not to exclude persons who have sexually abused children. Accordingly, we will hold our meetings on the basis of the following.
>
> 1. Anyone who has behaved so as to sexually abuse a child is prohibited from forming a new, ongoing relationship with any child in the context of the Meeting.
>
>    Friends recognize that children are naturally inclined toward adults and that children may on their own initiate contact with persons who are prohibited from having any relationships with children. In such instances, it is the responsibility of all adults in the Meeting to intervene immediately…
>
>    Also specifically prohibited for persons with histories of having sexually abused children are formal role relationships with children, such as teaching in First Day School, and less formal contact, such as providing transportation and assisting in recreational activities with children.
>
> 2. Rochester Friends Meeting calls into being a Committee for Oversight and Support of this Minute, which has the following duties.
>
>    a. The Committee will attend to the Meeting's efforts towards mutual care of each other, adults and children, and towards support of the prohibition of relationships between persons who have sexually abused children and children in the care of the Meeting. The Committee will periodically convene discussions about child sexual abuse, the experiences of adult survivors, the struggles toward recovery for offenders, and about sexual violence in our lives.
>
>    b. The Committee will receive disclosures and also receive and carefully review reports on our commitments to mutual care and on our adherence to the prohibition of relationships between children and persons who have sexually abused children.
>
>    The Committee will respond promptly to concerns from any source, reporting to Business Meeting, and informing specially connected persons, such as parents, as appropriate.
>
>    The Committee will invite consultation and evaluation of our experience living out this Minute at Business Meeting, in June 1993 and October 1993, or earlier, as it sees cause.
>
> — Minute on Child Sexual Abuse, Rochester (NY) Monthly Meeting, 3/93
