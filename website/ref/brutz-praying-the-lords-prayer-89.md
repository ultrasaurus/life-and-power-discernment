God of Courage
We call upon your Name and we commit
to living your Love every day.

Through the tough times of being
persecuted, continue giving us the
strength to follow you.

Forgive us when we just want to give up
as we forgive others for wanting to quit
also.

Help us to bless and pray for those
who persecute and to let go of our anger.

You are God in Christ, the Redeemer
and the Sustainer. Thank you.

Peace, peace

By Judy Brutz, Praying the Lord’s Prayer p.89
