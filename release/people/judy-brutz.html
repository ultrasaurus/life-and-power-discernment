Judy Brutz (Judith L. Brutz, 1940-2023, she/her) was a pioneer in researching Quaker family violence which led a lifelong public ministry. Judy Brutz held dual monthly meeting membership in Cleveland Meeting (Lake Erie Yearly Meeting and Ohio Conservative) and Des Moines First Friends Church (Iowa FUM). As an educator and therapist, she founded the Friends Family Service of Iowa Yearly Meeting. In remembrance, Friend Joan Liversidge wrote “Honoring an Elder in Our Work Against Interpersonal Violence: A Friend’s Recollection of Judy Brutz”

Her initial research, published in 1984, "Conflict Resolution in Quaker Families" is outlined in the paper abstract:

A survey of conflict resolution in Quaker families was made among congregations of the Lake Erie Yearly Meeting of the Religious Society of Friends, and the findings were compared to the national study on family violence. There were 130 male and 158 female respondents. The Conflict Tactics Scales were used to measure the index rates of overall and severe physical violence in three types of family dyads: spouse/spouse, parent/child, and sibling/sibling. In general, there were no significant differences in the violence rates between the Quaker respondents and the rates for the national study. However, Quaker fathers reported more acts of overall violence toward their children than did fathers nationally; and Quaker sibling violence, both overall and severe, was significantly higher than sibling violence rates reported nationally. The patterns of violence within the indices revealed striking differences between the Quaker sample and the national sample.

In her later life she wrote many spiritual books including biblical exploration and poetry. She wrote much original poetry using form and themes from the Lord’s Prayer.

God of Courage We call upon your Name and we commit to living your Love every day.

Through the tough times of being persecuted, continue giving us the strength to follow you.

Forgive us when we just want to give up as we forgive others for wanting to quit also.

Help us to bless and pray for those who persecute and to let go of our anger.

You are God in Christ, the Redeemer and the Sustainer. Thank you.

Peace, peace

By Judy Brutz, Praying the Lord’s Prayer p.89
