Reflections on Sexual Abuse Amongst Friends

“Addressing Sexual Abuse in Friends Meetings” was written and compiled by the working party on sexual abuse of New England Yearly Meeting Ministry and Counsel and published by New England Yearly Meeting in 1994 as a print booklet.

The preparation of these papers has been encouraged by the Committee on Ministry and Counsel of New England Yearly Meeting of Friends. This paper has been accepted as a working tool in Friends’ search for clarity and light as we face the reality of sexual abuse among us. It does not represent either our final or our corporate testimony.
